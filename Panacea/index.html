Panacea

More potions, a potion cauldron for mixing, and swords you can tip with brew effects.

Two new effects (with more on the way)

EffectWhat it doesWhere you find it
VulnerabilityYou take 20% more damage per level.Potion of the Coward and its variants, which gives you Speed but makes you frailer.
Frost ResistanceYou don't freeze. Powder snow and other freezing damage can't hurt you and freeze buildup stops.Frost Resistance potions.

The potion cauldron

A place to mix potions together. It holds up to 3 layers, and the liquid color blends to show what's inside.

What you doWhat happens
Use a potion on a cauldronAdds one layer (up to 3). The empty bottle is returned.
Use an empty glass bottle while not sneakingScoops one layer back out as a normal potion.
Sneak + use an empty bottle when 2-3 layers are insideMixes all layers together into a "Mixed Potion" that has every effect combined (with a small penalty to duration).
Use arrows on the mixed cauldronCreates tipped arrows with the mixed effects.
Use any sword on the mixed cauldronTips the sword. After this it applies the mixed effects on hit until charges run out.
How mixing works: when you combine, each effect keeps its strongest level but shares the duration. Mixing two 8-minute potions gives you each effect for about 4 minutes total, not 16.

Tipped swords

Dip any sword in a mixed cauldron to tip it. On each solid hit it applies all the stored effects to the target. Charges depend on how long the mixed potion lasts; short mixes give about 4 hits, long ones up to 32.
