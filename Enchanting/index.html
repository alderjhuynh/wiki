Arcanum

Enchanting reimagined: your gear never breaks and every enchantment choice matters.

Enchantments you won't find anymore

These have been removed to keep the pool focused. You won't get them from enchanting tables, loot, or the anvil.

EnchantmentWhy it's gone
Unbreaking & MendingNo durability to repair, so they're no longer needed.
SharpnessReplaced by more specialized damage options like Piercing and Rending.
Sweeping Edge, Knockback, PunchRemoved to simplify combat choices.
LoyaltyAll tridents return on their own now.
Impaling & DensityRemoved.
Binding & Vanishing CursesRemoved.
Frost Walker, Depth Strider, RespirationMovement and breathing handled differently by potions and biomes.
Luck of the SeaRemoved.
Soul SpeedRemoved.

Enchantments that changed

These are still in the game but work differently. Names and levels shown are what you'll see in-game.

EnchantmentItemsWhat it does now
Power → Might VBow & crossbowBoosts arrow damage for both bows and crossbows.
Piercing IV & Rending IVSwords & axesHelp your hits ignore shields or armor (respectively). You can only have one of the two.
Looting → Scavenger IIISwords & axesHits do a bit more damage from behind and slightly increase gear drops from mobs.
Bane of Arthropods V & Smite V & Fire Aspect IISwords & axesSame targets as before, tuned for the new balance.
Channeling IAny melee weaponSlows your swing a little, but striking an enemy calls down lightning with a burst of effects and sound.
Protection → Wardenspine IVChestplate onlyDamage reduction from behind, partial thorns, and attackers can be hit with Darkness.
Blast Protection IVChestplate onlyExtra blast resistance and less knockback from explosions.
Fire Protection IVChestplate onlyStrong fire resistance for chest armor.
Quick Charge VCrossbowFaster reload and arrows pierce further at higher levels.
Multishot → Scattershot IIICrossbowFires extra projectiles in a tighter spread.
Efficiency VToolsMines a bit faster, and now you must choose between speed, Fortune, or Silk Touch.

Still around but mostly untouched: Aqua Affinity, Feather Falling, Flame, Fortune, Infinity, Lunge, Lure, Riptide, Silk Touch, Swift Sneak, Thorns, and Wind Burst.
